TOMO-TV  ~ one episode a week, just like the good old days ~
==============================================================

How to start:
  Double-click TomoTV.exe

  Keep the "_internal" folder next to TomoTV.exe, TOMO-TV needs it.
  You can move the whole TomoTV folder anywhere you like (Documents, Desktop...).

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info", then "Run anyway". TOMO-TV isn't signed with a paid
  certificate, that's all.

Your channels and progress are saved in your user folder
(Settings inside the app shows exactly where), not in this folder,
so you can replace this folder with a newer version without losing anything.

More info and the source code: https://github.com/Tomasz-programista/Tomo
